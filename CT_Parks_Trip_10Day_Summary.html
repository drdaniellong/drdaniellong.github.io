Connecticut State Parks Trip

10-Day Itinerary Summary

This summary covers all ten days of the trip: five dog-friendly days close to Middletown and the Northwest Corner, followed by five days visiting parks with partial or no dog access. Each day pairs one or more state parks that sit near each other geographically. Full driving directions, maps, and hike details for each individual day are in the companion day-by-day documents.

*Day 4 flag: the 4h 45m hiking figure assumes walking the Windsor Locks Canal Trail's full 4.5-mile length one-way (9 miles round trip). Most visitors walk only a portion — walking just 2 miles round trip there instead would cut that day to roughly 4h 45m total, which is a much more realistic pace for a two-park day.

A few notes on how these numbers were built:

  • Driving time = Middletown → first park → any drive between that day's parks → last park → Middletown, using the drive times already in each day's document plus the return-trip times noted in the driving directions, with local/secondary road legs estimated around 25–35 mph.
  • Hiking time = the “normal-pace” times from the Suggested Hike sections already in the Word/HTML docs.
  • These totals don't include time actually spent inside museums, touring Gillette Castle or the forts, picnicking, swimming at Hammonasset, or meals — so treat this as the “must-have” time floor for each day, not the whole day's schedule. Days 4 and 9 are the most packed; Days 5 and 6 are the lightest, which lines up with them being designed as recovery/light days in the original itinerary.
