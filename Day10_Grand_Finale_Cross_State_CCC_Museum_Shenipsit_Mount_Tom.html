Day 10

Grand Finale Cross-State — CCC Museum (Shenipsit) & Mount Tom

Non-Dog-Friendly Day

Overview

The final day is a bookend to Day 1: another cross-state driving day, this time east-to-west. Start at the CCC Museum and Soapstone Mountain area of Shenipsit State Forest near Stafford/Somers, where an easy hike (or a seasonal drive) leads to a 40-foot observation tower with views into Massachusetts on a clear day, plus exhibits on the Civilian Conservation Corps. Then cross the state to Mount Tom State Park in Washington/Litchfield for one last hike to a stone observation tower overlooking Mount Tom Pond — a quiet, scenic way to close out the trip. Note there's no cell reception at Mount Tom, so download directions beforehand.

Pairing note: The one real outlier of the trip — these two aren't near each other or anything else, at roughly 45 miles apart.

Driving Directions

  1. From Middletown, take CT-9 N to I-91 N to Exit 47 (Rte 190) toward Somers/Stafford (about 30 miles).
  2. Follow CT-190 E/Gulf Rd to the Soapstone Mountain / CCC Museum area of Shenipsit State Forest.
  3. From Shenipsit, head west via CT-190/CT-140/CT-31 toward Rockville, then pick up CT-44 W toward Winsted (about 40 miles) — alternatively use I-84 W then CT-8 N to CT-118/CT-109.
  4. From the CT-44/CT-118 area near Winsted, take CT-118 S / CT-109 toward Washington Depot to reach Mount Tom Rd and the Mount Tom State Park entrance (about 15 miles).
  5. Return trip: CT-47/CT-109 to CT-202 E / I-84 E back to Middletown via CT-66 E (allow about 1 hour).
