About

Background

I hold a Ph.D. in Sociology from the University of Wisconsin–Madison, with graduate training in demography and economics, and I've spent 30 years analyzing large-scale administrative, longitudinal, and survey data — as a Senior Research Scientist at NWEA/HMH, a Research Scientist at UConn's Neag School of Education, Director of Research at Connecticut Voices for Children, Director of Quantitative Research at Research for Action, and an Assistant Professor of Sociology at Wesleyan University. Across those roles I've built the exact muscle institutional research demands: querying and reconciling administrative and student-level data systems, producing federal and state reporting, and translating statistical findings for non-technical stakeholders — school boards, legislators, journalists, and now, university leadership. My methodological toolkit — event history analysis, multilevel modeling, and quasi-experimental approaches such as propensity score matching — translates directly into studies of student retention, time-to-degree, and program effectiveness.

Reporting Pipeline

Simulated Banner-to-IPEDS Reporting Pipeline

An end-to-end simulated reporting workflow built to mirror how institutional research offices actually work: student and institutional data generated using Banner-standard naming conventions, queried and reconciled with Oracle SQL/PL scripts, and compiled into federally uploadable IPEDS survey files using sqlcl and the R package IPEDSuploadables. Demonstrates the full chain from raw administrative tables to submission-ready federal reporting.

Research

Promising Practices for Identifying Gifted Students from Underserved Populations

Ongoing research, presented at national research conferences, examining whether the severe underrepresentation of marginalized students in gifted and talented programs stems from bias in school identification procedures or from earlier opportunity gaps that surface as achievement disparities. This work includes the peer-reviewed study "Inequality at the Starting Line: Underrepresentation in Gifted Identification and Disparities in Early Achievement," published in AERA Open in 2023 and co-authored with D. Betsy McCoach and colleagues.
