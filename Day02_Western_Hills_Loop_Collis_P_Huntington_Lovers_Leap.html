Day 2

Western Hills Loop — Collis P. Huntington & Lovers Leap

Dog-Friendly Day

Overview

Today swings through Fairfield and Litchfield County's rolling western hills. Collis P. Huntington State Park is a beautiful, forested property with wide multi-use trails, small waterfalls, and open fields — a favorite for hiking with a dog. From Redding, head north to New Milford for Lovers Leap State Park, where roughly 4 miles of moderate trails follow the Housatonic River and cross the historic 1895 Lover's Leap truss bridge. Both parks are fully dog-friendly on leash. Wear real hiking shoes — sections of both parks are rocky, and Huntington can be muddy after rain.

Pairing note: The two parks sit about 14.8 miles apart — just under the trip's informal 15-mile pairing rule.

Driving Directions

  1. From Middletown, take CT-66 W to I-84 W (about 8 miles to the highway entrance).
  2. Follow I-84 W for roughly 30 miles to Exit 3 in Danbury, then follow signs for CT-53/Redding.
  3. Take CT-53 S / Sunset Hill Rd toward Redding center, then follow local roads (Sunset Hill Rd to Sunset Hill/Redding Rd) to Huntington State Park's entrance on Sunset Hill Road.
  4. From Huntington, head north on CT-7 toward New Milford (about 20 miles) — CT-7 hugs the Housatonic River most of the way.
  5. In New Milford, follow signs from Rte 7 to Pumpkin Hill Rd / Short Woods Rd for the Lovers Leap State Park entrance.
  6. Return trip: take CT-7 S to I-84 E back toward Middletown via CT-66 E (allow about 1 hour).
