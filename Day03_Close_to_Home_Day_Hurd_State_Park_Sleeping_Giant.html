Day 3

Close-to-Home Day — Hurd State Park & Sleeping Giant

Dog-Friendly Day

Overview

After two long driving days, today is a welcome break — both parks are within a short drive of Middletown, so you can sleep in a little. Hurd State Park sits right on the Connecticut River in East Hampton, with river-view trails, a swimming spot popular with dogs, and the well-known Split Rock formation. In the afternoon, head to Sleeping Giant State Park in Hamden for one of Connecticut's most rewarding hikes: the Tower Trail climbs steadily to an observation tower with panoramic views of New Haven and Long Island Sound. Note that Sleeping Giant's rocky trail surface can be tough on dog paws — consider dog booties or sticking to the lower, smoother sections if your dog is sensitive.

Pairing note: A lighter day by design — both parks are quick, close-to-home visits about 20 miles apart.

Driving Directions

  1. From Middletown, take CT-66 E / CT-151 toward East Hampton (about 6 miles).
  2. Follow signs to Hurd Park Rd for the Hurd State Park entrance.
  3. From Hurd, return to CT-66 W, then pick up CT-9 S toward Middletown and continue to I-91 S / Wilbur Cross Pkwy (CT-15) S toward New Haven (about 20 miles total).
  4. Take Wilbur Cross Pkwy S to Exit 61/62 area and follow signs for Mount Carmel Ave in Hamden.
  5. The main Sleeping Giant parking lot is on Mount Carmel Ave (Rte 10) just south of the Quinnipiac University area.
  6. Return trip: Rte 10 N / Wilbur Cross Pkwy N back to CT-9 N and home to Middletown (about 25 minutes).
