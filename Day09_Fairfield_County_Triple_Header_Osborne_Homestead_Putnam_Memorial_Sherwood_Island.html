Day 9

Fairfield County Triple-Header — Osborne Homestead, Putnam Memorial & Sherwood Island

Non-Dog-Friendly Day

Overview

The most ambitious single day of the trip, this loop threads together three very different parks. Start at Osborne Homestead Museum in Derby, a beautifully preserved early-1900s estate with formal gardens — a self-guided or docent tour takes about an hour. Next, Putnam Memorial State Park in Redding, Connecticut's first state park, commemorates a Revolutionary War winter encampment with a museum, monument, and easy walking loop. Finish at Sherwood Island State Park in Westport, a Long Island Sound beach park with a long promenade walk — note dogs are not permitted here. Given the driving involved, plan a reasonably early start.

Pairing note: A three-stop loop with each leg near the trip's 15-mile threshold.

Driving Directions

  1. From Middletown, take CT-9 S to I-95 S to CT-8 N toward Derby (about 30 miles).
  2. Take CT-8 N to Exit 15/Derby, then follow Hawthorne Ave to the Osborne Homestead Museum.
  3. From Derby, take CT-34/Merritt Pkwy (CT-15) S toward Redding (about 14–15 miles).
  4. Exit the Merritt Pkwy for Black Rock Tpke and follow signs to Putnam Memorial State Park.
  5. From Putnam Memorial, continue south on Black Rock Tpke to the Merritt Pkwy (CT-15) S, then to I-95 S toward Westport (about 14–15 miles).
  6. Take I-95 S to Exit 18 and follow the Sherwood Island Connector to the park entrance.
  7. Return trip: I-95 N to CT-9 N back to Middletown (allow about 1 hour 15 minutes).
