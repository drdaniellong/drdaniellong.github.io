Day 8

Farmington Valley Loop — Heublein Tower & Peoples State Forest

Non-Dog-Friendly Day

Overview

Today explores the Farmington Valley and Litchfield Hills foothills. Talcott Mountain State Park in Simsbury offers a popular (moderately steep at the start) 1-mile-each-way hike up to the historic Heublein Tower, with panoramic views across the valley on a clear day — the tower's upper floors are open seasonally. From there, drive northwest to Peoples State Forest in Barkhamsted, where well-marked trails along the West Branch of the Farmington River lead past waterfalls and scenic overlooks, and the Nature Museum has excellent exhibits on the CCC's role in building the park.

Pairing note: A Farmington Valley loop, about 12.4 miles apart.

Driving Directions

  1. From Middletown, take CT-9 N to I-91 N to Hartford, then I-84 W to Exit 39 (Simsbury/CT-10).
  2. Follow CT-10 N to CT-185 W toward Simsbury center, then follow signs to Summit Ridge Dr / Talcott Mountain State Park.
  3. From Talcott Mountain, head north and west via CT-185/CT-179 toward Barkhamsted (about 15 miles).
  4. Follow East River Rd along the Farmington River to the Peoples State Forest Nature Museum entrance.
  5. Return trip: retrace CT-179/CT-44 E to I-84 E/I-91 S back to Middletown (about 1 hour).
