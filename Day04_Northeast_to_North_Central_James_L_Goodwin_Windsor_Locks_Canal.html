Day 4

Northeast to North-Central — James L. Goodwin & Windsor Locks Canal

Dog-Friendly Day

Overview

Today covers more ground, connecting the quiet Quiet Corner in the northeast with the Connecticut River valley up near the Massachusetts line. James L. Goodwin State Forest in Hampton offers an easy, mostly flat network of trails through woods and around Pine Acres Lake, plus a native plant garden — a gentle morning walk for you and the dog. Then drive north and west to Windsor Locks Canal State Park, a flat, paved 4.5-mile (one-way) trail along the Connecticut River and its historic canal — easy, scenic, and dog-friendly, though note the steel grate bridge near the trailhead (there's a wooden dog-walk panel, but some dogs are hesitant).

Pairing note: The biggest stretch of the dog-friendly stretch, at roughly 28 miles apart — pairing them saves a full day.

Driving Directions

  1. From Middletown, take CT-66 E toward Columbia, then CT-6 E toward Willimantic and on toward Hampton (about 30 miles).
  2. Follow signs from Rte 6/Rte 97 to Potter Rd for the James L. Goodwin State Forest entrance and visitor center.
  3. From Goodwin, head back west on CT-6 to I-84 W, then north on I-291/I-91 N toward Windsor Locks/Suffield (roughly 45 miles).
  4. Take I-91 N to Exit 45 and follow local roads (Canal Rd) in Suffield to the Windsor Locks Canal State Park trailhead.
  5. Return trip: I-91 S back to CT-9 S / CT-66 to Middletown (about 40 minutes).
