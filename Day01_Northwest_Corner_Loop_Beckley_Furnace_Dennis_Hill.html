Day 1

Northwest Corner Loop — Beckley Furnace & Dennis Hill

Dog-Friendly Day

Overview

Today is the long haul into Connecticut's Northwest Corner, so plan for a full day mostly spent driving and hiking rather than a leisurely morning. Start early to make the most of daylight. Beckley Furnace is a well-preserved 19th-century iron blast furnace site with an easy, flat trail along the river and picnic tables — a relaxed way to stretch your legs after the drive. From there it's a short 6.5-mile hop to Dennis Hill, where the Gazebo Trail (about 2.5 miles, easy-to-moderate) climbs to a stone gazebo with sweeping views over the Litchfield Hills. Both stops are fully dog-friendly on leash. Pack a picnic lunch — both parks have tables — and bring water for the dog since neither site has reliable public fountains.

Pairing note: Beckley Furnace and Dennis Hill are only 6.5 miles apart, making this the tightest loop of the whole trip — it's just the farthest one from home.

Driving Directions

  1. From Middletown, take CT-9 N to I-91 N toward Hartford (about 10 miles).
  2. Continue on I-91 N to Exit 34 / CT-44 W in Hartford/Bloomfield.
  3. Follow CT-44 W for approximately 45 miles, passing through Avon, Canton, New Hartford, and Winsted.
  4. In Norfolk, watch for CT-272 N; Dennis Hill State Park's entrance is on the left about 1.5 miles north of the Norfolk town green — consider stopping here first or last depending on traffic.
  5. Continue on CT-44 W from Norfolk toward Canaan (about 6 miles).
  6. In East Canaan, turn onto Lower Road (CT-44/Lower Rd junction) to reach Beckley Furnace Industrial Monument.
  7. Return trip: retrace CT-44 E to I-91 S back to Middletown (allow about 1h 30m for the return drive).
