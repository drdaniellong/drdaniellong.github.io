Day 5

Shoreline Solo — Bluff Point State Park

Dog-Friendly Day

Overview

The last dog-friendly day of the trip is a single, unhurried stop at Bluff Point State Park in Groton — one of the highest-rated parks on the whole list. Wide, well-groomed multi-use trails wind through coastal forest out to a rocky point with sweeping Long Island Sound views and access to Bluff Point Beach and Mumford Cove. Give yourselves the whole day here: there are several miles of trails, and the coastline sections make for a great slow exploration with a dog. Out-of-state visitors should note a $12 parking fee applies to non-residents.

Pairing note: A true solo day — there's no dog-friendly park close enough nearby to pair with Bluff Point.

Driving Directions

  1. From Middletown, take CT-9 S to I-95 S toward New London/Groton (about 30 miles).
  2. Take I-95 S to Exit 88 (Groton).
  3. Follow signs south on Depot Rd to the Bluff Point State Park parking area.
  4. Return trip: Depot Rd back to I-95 N, then I-95 N to CT-9 N and home to Middletown (about 50 minutes).
