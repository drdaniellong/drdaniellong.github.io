Day 7

River to Shore — Gillette Castle & Hammonasset Beach

Non-Dog-Friendly Day

Overview

This is one of the most scenic days of the trip and stays close to home. Gillette Castle State Park in East Haddam sits high above the Connecticut River, with a whimsical stone castle (interior tours are ticketed and time-slotted — book ahead if you want to go inside), grounds, trails, and a picnic area with river views. In the afternoon, head south to Hammonasset Beach State Park in Madison, Connecticut's largest shoreline park, with over two miles of beach, a boardwalk-style Meigs Point nature area, and a nature center. Good weather makes this a great beach-and-castle combo day.

Pairing note: A scenic Connecticut-River-to-shoreline combo, about 12.7 miles apart.

Driving Directions

  1. From Middletown, take CT-9 S to Exit 6/CT-82 toward East Haddam (about 12 miles).
  2. Follow CT-82 E across the swing bridge, then CT-148 to River Rd for the Gillette Castle State Park entrance.
  3. From Gillette Castle, return to CT-9 S and continue to I-95 S toward Madison (about 20 miles).
  4. Take I-95 S to Exit 62 and follow signs on Boston Post Rd (US-1) to the Hammonasset Beach State Park entrance.
  5. Return trip: I-95 N to CT-9 N back to Middletown (about 30 minutes).
