Day 6

Groton/New London History Day — Fort Griswold & Fort Trumbull

Non-Dog-Friendly Day

Overview

Leave the dog home today for the trip's lightest-driving, history-heaviest day. Fort Griswold Battlefield State Park in Groton preserves the earthworks of a Revolutionary War fort, with a climbable monument tower and small museum. A short drive (and bridge crossing) takes you to Fort Trumbull State Park in New London, a striking 19th-century fort on the Thames River with a visitor center/museum, walking trails, and a pier popular for watching fishing boats and Coast Guard vessels. Budget 1–2 hours at each site; the Fort Trumbull museum has a small entrance fee.

Pairing note: Practically next door to each other — about 1 mile apart across the Thames River.

Driving Directions

  1. From Middletown, take CT-9 S to I-95 S toward New London/Groton (about 30 miles).
  2. Take I-95 S to Exit 86 and follow signs to Monument St / Park Ave for Fort Griswold Battlefield State Park.
  3. From Fort Griswold, cross the Thames River via the Gold Star Memorial Bridge (I-95 S) to New London, Exit 84.
  4. Follow signs to Walbach St for the Fort Trumbull State Park visitor center and parking.
  5. Return trip: I-95 N back to CT-9 N to Middletown (about 45 minutes).
